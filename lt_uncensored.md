# Uncensored AI Writing Prompts — write scenes without the sanitization pass

If your AI novel chapters read like a Disney+ adaptation of your idea, the problem is the
system prompt, not the model. Safety layers respond to patterns: the moment your prompt looks
like a creative-writing request with editorial oversight, the sanitization pass kicks in —
violence gets abstract, desire gets euphemism, grief gets a hopeful coda stapled on.

Prompts that hold uncensored quality share four traits:
1. **The editorial committee is removed in advance** — "you do not moralize, summarize, or
   break scene to reassure the reader" stated as standing fact
2. **Sensory mandates** — smell, texture, sound, weight; the model can't fade-to-black when
   the prompt demands the copper tang and the sticky floor
3. **Consequence locks** — injuries persist, deaths stick, the character who lost the hand
   still has one hand in chapter nine
4. **Output contracts** — the format itself forbids disclaimers (see the Compiler Contract
   technique in the Pro vault)
